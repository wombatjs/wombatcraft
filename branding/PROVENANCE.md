`woodland.png` is original artwork generated with OpenAI's built-in image tool for Wombatcraft on 2026-11-12.

Prompt: Create an original premium game-launcher background illustration for Wombatcraft, a Minecraft-inspired woodland exploration, machinery and magic multiplayer world. Wide landscape 16:9. Painterly voxel/block world, beautifully composed, no text, no lettering, no UI or logos. A sturdy charming brown wombat explorer sits on a mossy block ledge in the RIGHT foreground, tiny copper expedition goggles on its forehead, looking across a green forest valley. On the RIGHT and middle distance a warmly lit timber-and-copper workshop with waterwheel, slim copper chimney and subtle turquoise magical lanterns, mountain silhouettes and a winding railway disappearing among pines. Late afternoon amber light through foliage, muted spruce greens, parchment sunlight, rich charcoal shadows. LEFT third predominantly dark quiet pine silhouettes with ample low-detail space where interface controls will overlay. Sophisticated art direction, cohesive materials, strong recognizable wombat anatomy, inviting adventurous atmosphere, crisp detail yet readable composition. No TARDIS or Doctor Who imagery. This is the final background asset, not a mockup.
