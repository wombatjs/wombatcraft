# Wombatcraft

An approachable but deep multiplayer Minecraft modpack. Players grow from a frontier camp into engineers, spellcasters, explorers and settlement builders. Major mods have complete progression branches, with addons integrated where they become useful. TARDIS is an optional exploration branch, not the pack's identity.

Audience: friends and public players, including beginners who want clear direction and experienced players who want sustained goals. The launcher must make account sign-in, installation, updates and joining the official server understandable without developer knowledge.

Brand: Wombatcraft. Warm woodland copper, parchment, charcoal and restrained magical turquoise. A sturdy wombat emblem, expedition-map details and strong readable typography. Avoid neon dashboards, generic purple gradients and verbose explanatory copy.

Player prose: brief, confident and grounded in the world. Use practical instructions when needed. Keep test notes, implementation explanations and development caveats in documentation rather than quests.

Operations: user's home server, existing public proxy, Pterodactyl. Preserve FiveM and other services; FiveM may be stopped if capacity requires it. Preserve Fabric backup. Public multiplayer uses licensed accounts and online-mode authentication.
