# Wombatcraft

An approachable but deep multiplayer Minecraft modpack. Players grow from a frontier camp into engineers, spellcasters, explorers and settlement builders. Major mods have complete progression branches, with addons integrated where they become useful. TARDIS is an optional exploration branch, not the pack's identity.
